# FocusPulse Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** March 21, 2026
_Last updated: March 21, 2026_
**Version:** 1.0.0

FocusPulse ("we," "our," or "us") is a Pomodoro and focus timer app for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your data.

## Information We Collect

FocusPulse does not collect any personal data. All data stays on your device.

### Data Stored Locally
- Focus session history, productivity statistics, and timer preferences are stored locally on your device using Room database.
- No account or sign-in is required.

## How We Use Your Information

- Display your session history and productivity statistics within the app.
- Remember your timer and notification preferences.

We do not transmit any data to external servers. We do not sell, rent, or share your data.

## Storage and Retention

- All data is stored exclusively on your device.
- Data is retained until you clear app data or uninstall the app.
- No SudarshanTechLabs server stores your data.

## Data Security

- Local data is protected by Android's built-in storage encryption.

## Permissions Used

- **POST_NOTIFICATIONS** (Android 13+): Required to show session start/end and break reminders.
- **FOREGROUND_SERVICE**: Required to keep the focus timer running reliably when the app is in the background.
- **DO_NOT_DISTURB_ACCESS**: Used to automatically enable Do Not Disturb during focus sessions (optional, user-controlled).

## Your Rights and Controls

- Delete all data by clearing app data in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.
- Revoke Do Not Disturb access in Android Settings → Apps → FocusPulse → Permissions at any time.

## Children's Privacy

FocusPulse is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect new features or legal requirements. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
